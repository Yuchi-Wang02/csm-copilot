THREE-MINUTE CUSTOMER STORY

0:00–0:30 — Frame the problem.
Show Lantern Games and the snapshot label. Explain that a publisher wants to
launch but Android evidence is incomplete. State what decision the CSM needs
to move forward. Do not claim this is a real customer or measured business gain.

0:30–1:10 — Trace the decision.
In Evidence, expand "Trace checklist decisions to source records" and inspect
"Non-test device verification". Contrast its recorded failure with unknown
currency approval. Explain why a fresh device test and a missing approval
confirmation are different requests. An iOS check does not establish Android
readiness, and a complete scoped checklist is not launch authorization.

1:10–1:50 — Prepare the next action.
Open Follow-up, then Handoff. Show a concrete request and a missing intake
field. The handoff needs SDK version, time window and redacted logs; it retains
the customer's recorded region and reproduction details. Explain which owner
needs which information. A generated draft sends nothing and does not count
as a new customer contact.

1:50–2:30 — Inspect an AI mistake.
In the qwen4-batch recording, open "Check the claim against the recorded result".
Contrast the model's "iOS's full compliance" with the scoped checklist result.
The model completed four calls, but its wording overstated what they proved.
Explain the division of work: deterministic tools produce workflow results;
an optional model selects tools and writes an explanation that still needs review.

2:30–3:00 — Explain your contribution and learning.
Describe the project direction you selected and the substantial AI assistance
used for implementation. Give one decision or code path you can explain in
your own words. If you have completed a practice or source-change experiment,
show your actual prediction, observation and reflection. Otherwise describe
the next exercise as planned work. End here and invite a question.
